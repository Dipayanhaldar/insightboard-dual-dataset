InsightBoard Privacy Notice

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This notice describes the data handling currently documented for InsightBoard. It must be reviewed and updated if the product's data flows, hosting, analytics or providers change.

Data you provide

When account and saved-report features are enabled, account details and password hashes are stored by the InsightBoard backend. Saved report snapshots are stored in the configured database. Do not upload confidential or regulated data unless you are authorised to do so and have reviewed the applicable service terms.

Datasets and files

Dashboard imports and the dual-dataset comparison workflow process raw dataset rows in your browser. The comparison workflow does not send those raw rows to the InsightBoard API. Other features may have different data flows; review the relevant feature before submitting information.

AI and voice providers

AI Provider Hub and Gnani Voice Studio use bring-your-own-key requests. Provider credentials are handled in the browser session and sent to the provider selected by the user for the requested operation. The selected provider processes the request under its own privacy terms. Do not enter a key on a shared device.

Hosting and service operations

InsightBoard is hosted using third-party infrastructure. Those providers may process technical information such as IP address, request metadata and diagnostic logs as part of operating and securing the service. Database and hosting configuration may vary between environments.

Retention and deletion

Account and saved-report data remain in the configured database until deleted through supported product controls or by the service operator. Browser-held data may remain in browser storage until cleared. Contact the publisher through the support contact shown on the Microsoft Store listing for privacy requests.

Third-party services

InsightBoard may connect to external AI providers and hosting services. Their own terms and privacy notices govern their processing. InsightBoard does not control those providers.

Changes and contact

This notice may be updated as the product changes. For privacy questions, support requests or data-related requests, contact the publisher at Contact.dipayanhaldar@gmail.com.
